The logistics industry is experiencing a fundamental transformation driven by automation and data analytics. Autonomous vehicles are being tested for last-mile delivery in several German cities, with promising results in controlled environments. Warehouse robotics adoption has accelerated, with companies reporting 200-300% improvements in picking speed. Real-time tracking and predictive routing algorithms have reduced delivery times and fuel consumption significantly. However, the human workforce remains essential for complex decision-making, customer interaction, and handling exceptions. Companies that successfully combine automation with skilled human workers are outperforming those that pursued full automation strategies.
