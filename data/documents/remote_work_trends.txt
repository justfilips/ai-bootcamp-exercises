Remote work policies continue to evolve as companies find their balance between flexibility and collaboration. A recent survey of 2,000 German companies revealed that 73% now offer hybrid work arrangements, up from just 12% before 2020. Employee satisfaction scores have improved notably in organizations that allow at least two remote days per week. However, concerns about team cohesion, mentoring of junior staff, and innovation through spontaneous interaction persist. Some companies have started implementing "collaboration weeks" where entire teams gather in-person for focused project work, while maintaining remote flexibility for individual tasks.
