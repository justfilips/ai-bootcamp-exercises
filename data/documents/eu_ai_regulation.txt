The European Union's AI Act represents the world's first comprehensive regulatory framework for artificial intelligence. Coming into full effect in 2026, the regulation classifies AI systems by risk level and imposes strict requirements on high-risk applications in areas such as healthcare, law enforcement, and employment. Companies deploying AI must now ensure transparency, maintain detailed documentation, and implement human oversight mechanisms. While critics argue the regulation may slow innovation and increase compliance costs, supporters believe it will build public trust and create a competitive advantage for European AI companies that meet the highest standards of safety and accountability.
