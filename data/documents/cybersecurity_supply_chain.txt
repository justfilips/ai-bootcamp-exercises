Cybersecurity threats targeting supply chains have increased dramatically over the past three years. The SolarWinds attack demonstrated how a single compromised vendor can expose thousands of organizations simultaneously. Companies are now investing heavily in zero-trust architectures and vendor risk assessment programs. Despite these efforts, a recent report found that 62% of organizations experienced at least one supply chain attack in the past year. The shortage of qualified cybersecurity professionals makes the situation worse, with an estimated 3.5 million unfilled positions globally. Organizations are increasingly turning to AI-powered security tools to supplement their human teams, though this introduces new dependencies and potential vulnerabilities.
