The rise of artificial intelligence in manufacturing has transformed how factories operate across Europe. Predictive maintenance systems now monitor equipment in real-time, reducing unplanned downtime by up to 40%. Companies that have adopted AI-driven quality control report significant improvements in defect detection rates. However, the transition requires substantial investment in both technology and workforce training. Many small and medium enterprises still struggle to justify the initial costs, despite clear long-term benefits. Industry experts predict that by 2028, over 60% of manufacturing processes will incorporate some form of AI assistance.
