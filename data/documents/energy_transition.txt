Germany's renewable energy transition is facing unexpected challenges as the country phases out nuclear power while simultaneously trying to reduce fossil fuel dependence. Solar and wind capacity have grown substantially, but grid infrastructure has not kept pace. Energy storage remains a bottleneck, with current battery technology unable to bridge multi-day periods of low wind and cloud cover. The government has committed €20 billion to grid modernization, but projects face lengthy approval processes and local opposition. Meanwhile, industrial energy costs remain among the highest in Europe, putting pressure on manufacturers to relocate to regions with cheaper power.
